All six reports in `bugsarcturus.zip` were considered. The two sawblade reports describe the same missing attack. Existing laser endpoint work is documented separately in `notes/todo/5-4.md`.

Spawns are boss-owned, reward-suppressed, navigation-projected, and session-guarded. No arbitrary recurring-wave or fixed-count replacement was added.
- Aerial phase: chunk 569 (`Abilities/0x48FDF82A.lua`, SHA-256 `7c250fb865d49c9780d2dd937de30b183a7401264de41129c1c6075bc775f86c`) supplies cooldown 30, invulnerability after one second, twelve drops separated by 0.5 seconds, two-second post-drop pause, landing hit after 0.2 seconds, then 1.08-second recovery. The first drop occurs at 1.5 seconds; landing begins at nine seconds and normal combat resumes at 10.28 seconds. Ordinary casts are suspended by a feature-owned launch state. Visibility is restored on landing, including when the source died during the phase. A failed individual phase logs the failure without canceling the scheduled reveal/resume.
- Missile selection follows the script's five-slot random choice: available live heroes receive aimed drops; missing slots scatter around the boss within twelve units. Chunk 444 supplies launch height 30, speed 15, distance 31, radius four, 12–18 energy Technology damage and coefficient 0.05. Falling projectile and RocketShadow objects are explicitly retired at impact. Impact damage uses current target poses, shared mitigation, critical resolution, and damage authority rather than directly changing health.

## Compatibility decisions and verification

Landing now pushes surviving targets after an accepted hit using the shared navigation-clipped forced-movement path. The two-unit displacement is an explicit conservative fallback: the recovered launch knockback GUID `0x0139de24` is also used by deployment, but its displacement definition is not recovered. Avoided/protected hits and defeated targets are not pushed. Missile hits now emit the authored per-target `citadel_boss_missile_hit.ServerEventDef`, separately from the ground explosion. Missile impact failure independently hard-stops/deletes the shadow and deletes the projectile; stale-session callbacks do not publish old object IDs.

Ranked turret setup is now implemented using the imported `CitadelBossTurret_v1/v2` profiles. Turret chunk 93 (`Abilities/0x4BEC2E07.lua`, SHA-256 `a9ab9624797bfaad9ea93b0c9143b2ab8e895610004fd8168daba1081957b485`) supplies `TurretLaserSweep`, half-second line-damage checks, 40-unit beam length, and beam/muzzle/tip/hit effects. Server-owned sweeping markers share the same navigation-clipped segment with hit checks. The turret becomes vulnerable one second into launch until the landing hit, can be replaced on a later launch after destruction, and explicitly stops effects and removes objects on retirement. Cleanup uses captured zone identity rather than relying on the boss controller remaining registered. The 24-second horizontal rotation is a conservative fallback, not recovered native `OrbitingBeamMarker` motion.

Twin Laser now creates separate right/left `SweepingBeamMarker` objects and links the corresponding beam/muzzle/ground effects separately. Chunk 565 (`Abilities/0x06C94672.lua`, SHA-256 `00c4c69717d3c1d4f71f8dcebe8f24907b67fd186485432960496982cab12088`) places them three units to either side of the cast-time target, holds for 0.1 seconds per metre with speed 0.001, then accelerates inward at 24 units/second squared for 0.5 seconds. Server endpoints update every 50 ms; damage uses those same endpoints and scaled right/left muzzle offsets (±0.6, 1.1, 1.2). Targets must have a clear path from the source. End animation starts with the inward sweep; cleanup removes both markers without restarting that animation or clearing a newer cast. The cast no longer snaps both lasers onto the moving target.

Missiles now join the shared hostile-projectile registry and poll actual motion every 50 ms. Freeze, gravity, speed changes, reflection/destruction, and session retirement affect the missile before impact. Ground markers follow deflected flight, and impact uses the resulting ground position and elapsed time rather than the old fixed two-second deadline. Navigation rejection retires an unsafe missile without damage. Missile knockback uses a two-unit navigation-clipped push away from the explosion after accepted damage, matching the conservative landing fallback; native `impulseStrength=12000` still lacks a verified force/mass conversion, so exact native impulse parity is not claimed.

Server policy uses the original encounter center as the landing destination, a 30-second first-launch delay, and explicit hiding at the authored two-second visibility boundary; it does not teleport Arcturus onto the victim. Navigation rejection skips an unsafe drop/spawn. The underlying native helper's exact center-placement tolerance and full retail selector arbitration are not recovered.

No shipped files were changed. Formatting and diff checks only: no builds, compilation, or tests were run. All new behavior still requires an in-client encounter run, including AI-zoo reproduction and ranked encounters.
