# Campaign roster observations

These are human-supplied retail roster observations. They constrain population
themes, but noun identities must still be cross-checked against each level's
prepared director pools before server implementation.

## 1-1

| Role | Enemy | Theme |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minion | Space Barracuda | Quantum |
| Shooter | Cannonator | Quantum |
| Minion | Reparatron | Cyber |
| Lieutenant | Haster | Quantum |
| Lieutenant | Decelerator | Quantum |
| Lieutenant | Invincitron | Cyber |

Population policy selected by the project owner:

- Floor one uses Cannonator/Reparatron pairs around Invincitron lieutenant
  points.
- Each later floor independently chooses either that theme or Space Barracuda
  pairs around a randomly selected Haster/Decelerator lieutenant.
- A floor selects one random elite point, then the furthest remaining elite
  point, places two to four minion pairs near each, and fills random remaining
  minion points to roughly 15 total actors.

Final challenge: Horde, then Illust: the Accelerator (mini-boss).

## Stationary objects

- Gravitic Regulator assumes multiple shapes and, when destroyed, deals low
  damage to nearby enemies.
- Gravitic Stabilizer creates a time bubble that slows heroes. When destroyed,
  it damages and knocks back nearby enemies.
