# Campaign 6-2 enemy-description audit

## Result

All fifteen `scaldron_1` families match their English long descriptions. Hover-Bots connect to peers with damaging beams; Nova self-detonates into a heavy burn; Sinkhole, Scaldron Dog, and Death Mines retain their audited mechanics. Undermind, Pouncing Stalker, Shielded Grenadier, Acid Shell, Molten Crawler, Rootmob, Grappling Pulsar, Warp Spawner, Raytheoid, and Laser Tank reuse earlier audits. No production change was required.
