# Campaign 4-2 enemy-description audit

## Result

The fifteen `infinity_3` families now match their English long descriptions. Voltroid charges nearby Voltroids to five stacks and then discharges its devastating Energy attack. Suppression Mechanoid already launched stunning/dazing homing missiles and followed with melee; this pass added its missing ten-unit communications-jamming silence aura. Citadel ranged, Reconstructionist, Shielded Grenadier, Pouncing Stalker, Ragetusk, Pterodyne, Menace Weed, Tentacler, Grappling Pulsar, Reparatron, Pack Brawler, Hypno Mantis, and Lightning Juggernaut reuse previously audited behavior.

## Authority

Decoded long descriptions are `0x18484896` for Voltroid's ally charge and `0xd8707a3f`, `0xc372a4a8`, and `0x7482e58a` for Suppression Mechanoid's aura, missile, and melee phases.
